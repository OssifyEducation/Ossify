How duels work
Find a colleague
Search by name, @username or exact email address and send a challenge. You play first, then they have 3 days to reply.
Same 10 questions
You both answer the same 10 randomly selected questions from the full bank.
Speed matters
Higher score wins. If tied, the faster time wins — the clock runs from when you start your turn.
Win XP and badges
Every win earns 25 XP (10 each for an exact draw). Your first win unlocks the Duel Victor badge (+75 XP).
