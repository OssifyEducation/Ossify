MSRA · Paper 1

Professional Dilemmas

Practise the situational judgement paper as it is set: you answer as an F2 doctor, and each scenario asks you either to rank four or five actions or to choose the three that together best resolve the situation.

Ranking

Place every option from most to least appropriate. You don't need a perfect order to score well: each option earns marks according to how close it is to the key.

Up to 4 marks per option · 20 per scenario
Multiple choice

Choose exactly three of eight actions. Each keyed action you select earns marks; there is no negative marking.

4 marks per keyed action · 12 per scenario
How Ossify scores your answers

The mark maxima above come from NHS England's published MSRA practice paper. The exact credit for a "near miss" in ranking questions isn't published, so Ossify uses a transparent practice model: 4 marks for an option in its keyed position, 3 if it is one place out, 2 if two places, 1 if three and 0 if four. Your score is an educational guide, not an official MSRA result or a prediction of your scaled score.

The real paper has 50 scenarios in 95 minutes, roughly 1 minute 54 seconds each. Topic Mode is untimed so you can think each one through; the mock exams are timed like the real paper.
